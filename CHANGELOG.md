# Changelog

All notable changes to Nabbit for Android are documented in this file.
This project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

- Cards on the to-do list show their whole time instead of cutting off
  after the day

- Quick add shows two notes to a row on the small widget, not one

- The bunny note icon, same as on iPhone, in every launcher shape and
  in themed icons

- Salty (16+) and Sweary (18+) tones: tap one and Google Play asks
  once to share your age range. If it's shared and old enough, it
  unlocks; otherwise it stays locked and Plain and Sassy are there

- Nabbit stays free, with no ads and no subscription. If it helps you,
  Settings > Keep Nabbit going takes an optional tip toward the upkeep.
  A tip doesn't unlock anything

- Now and then, right after you clear the whole day, Nabbit may ask for
  a Google Play review. Never while you're making a reminder, and at
  most once every few months

- Two home screen widgets. Next up shows what's due next with Done,
  15 min, and Tonight right on it, plus the two after it when it's
  wide. Quick add holds your hot keys and verbs, so one tap starts a
  reminder before the thought is gone

- The to-do list: tap the eye at the top of Home for everything coming
  up as notes on a board, slipped first, then today, tomorrow, and each
  day after. Cards or a plain list, tap a note when it's done (with
  Undo), press and hold for 15 min or Tonight. The screen stays on

- The line at the bottom of Home, with Undo, sits above the navigation
  bar instead of under it

- Pick day & time is quicker: pick the day on the calendar, then tap a
  time and it's saved. No clock to work through, and Another time
  takes an exact minute

- A win you put back on the list rings again: if its time has passed,
  it comes back due in 15 minutes instead of waiting for you to open
  Nabbit

- A time from Pick day & time rings at exactly that minute, even when
  it's only a minute or two away

- Home says so when a phone setting would make reminders late or
  quiet (notifications, Alarms & reminders, or full-screen alerts),
  with a button to turn it on

- Picking Sassy in Settings now shows it as picked

- The mic button's words are bigger and shorter, and the prompt stays
  on one line so it reads at a glance

- First Android version: make a reminder in three taps or by saying it,
  get a full-screen alarm with Done, 15 min, and Tonight, then up to
  three nudges that get more direct before Nabbit goes quiet

- Today, Coming up, and Wins today on Home, with hot keys for the
  reminders you make a lot and a little celebration on every Done

- Five themes, Plain and Sassy tones, your own Morning and Tonight
  times, Alarm or Notification for the first alert, and a checklist
  for the Android settings that let Nabbit ring on time
